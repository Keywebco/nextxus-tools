Honest limits — what this kit does NOT do

Read this before you sell it to anyone

  • It cannot answer a phone. There is no phone number here, no voice, no 24/7 answering. A phone number is a rented service, not a file. That is the one job in Votel's list that genuinely needs a paid service.
  • It cannot send SMS or email. The follow-up sequences on this page are text to copy and send yourself, not automation. No pacing, no win-back trigger, no drip timing. You, or your own free email, send them.
  • It cannot book into a live calendar by itself. The booking page is a form the owner reads. For live booking, paste in your own free calendar link (Google Calendar, Calendly free tier) — the template has a place for it.
  • It has no CRM, no database, no server. Leads are not stored anywhere by this kit. The intake form gives you the fields; where you keep the answers (a notebook, a spreadsheet, your own email) is your choice, and your responsibility.
  • The chat demo below is a demo. It answers from the FAQ sheet you write, by keyword. It is not a real AI. For a real chat, use a Federation guide with your own API key (see the agent map), or the Roger Sim / Val Companion pages — and know that those rent a model through an API, the same as Votel does.
  • No income is promised. No client is guaranteed. No agency at $200,000 a month is quoted here, because those are other people's agencies. What this kit promises is the files, and that they cost $0.

What to use instead, free, for the things this kit cannot do: for a phone number, a pay-as-you-go number from a carrier (about $1/month for the number, minutes metered — that is the honest price, and it is the same meter Votel resells); for SMS, your own phone; for email, your own email account; for live booking, Google Calendar appointment schedules (free) or Calendly free tier; for a real AI chat, a Federation guide (DeepSeek default, your own key stays in your browser) or DeepAI Chat as a link-out (outside service, your own DeepAI account).

How to resell it under your own brand

  1. Build a snapshot above. Download the kit (.txt) and the booking page (.html).
  2. Replace "Your Business" with the client's business name, and the services, hours, and phone with theirs. That is the whole rebrand — the files are plain text and plain HTML.
  3. Host the booking page free on GitHub Pages, or print the kit and hand it to the client. The files are yours; there is no licence fee, no royalty, no monthly charge.
  4. Tell the client, in plain language, what it does and what it does not do — use the Honest limits section above, word for word if you like. A client who knows the limits will not come back angry.
  5. Charge for your work — setup, writing their FAQ sheet, testing their calls — not for the files. The files are free. Your time is not.
